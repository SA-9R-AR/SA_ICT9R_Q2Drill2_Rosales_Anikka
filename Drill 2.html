The Umbrella Academy is a private educational institution established by the Umbrella Corporation, specializing in science, medicine, biotechnology, and advanced research. It provides students with modern facilities, highly trained instructors, and opportunities to develop skills in scientific innovation, healthcare, and technology. The academy aims to produce future scientists, researchers, and medical professionals.
